# Voice guide

Starting points for writing lines. A character's own `voice:` notes in the profile always win.

## Alliance races

| Race | Register | Typical words and greetings | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human | Plain, practical, often wry | "Light be with you", "Well met", Stormwind, the King, the harvest | Elvish or orcish phrases |
| Dwarf | Hearty, loud, warm | "Aye", "lad/lass", ale, stone, forge, Ironforge, "By my beard!" | Flowery speech |
| Night Elf | Formal, unhurried, ancient | "Ishnu-alah" (hello), "Elune-adore" (goodbye), Elune, moonlight, the wilds, centuries | Contractions in solemn lines, haste |
| Gnome | Quick, curious, technical | Gadgets, numbers, "Fascinating!", Gnomeregan, tinkering | Slow solemnity |
| Draenei (if available) | Calm, gentle, accented formality | "The Light guide you", "Archenon poros" (good fortune), the Naaru | Crudeness |

## Horde races

| Race | Register | Typical words and greetings | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orc | Blunt, proud, short sentences | "Lok'tar ogar" (victory or death), "Throm-ka" (well met), honour, blood and thunder | Hesitation, frills |
| Troll | Laid back, playful, rhythmic | "Stay away from da voodoo", "mon", loa, the spirits | Formal stiffness |
| Tauren | Slow, wise, nature-minded | "Earth Mother guide you", "Walk with the Earth Mother", the winds, the herd | Haste, cruelty |
| Undead (Forsaken) | Dry, morbid, sardonic | "Dark Lady watch over you", decay, the grave, bitterness | Cheerfulness without irony |
| Blood Elf (if available) | Refined, proud, a little haughty | "Bal'a dash, malanore" (greetings, traveller), Quel'Thalas, the Sunwell, magic | Coarse words |

## Class flavour

- **Warrior:** steel, drills, the next fight, short commands.
- **Paladin / Priest:** the Light, duty, mercy, judgement.
- **Mage:** arcane theory, precision, faint superiority.
- **Warlock:** whispers, bargains, menace kept polite.
- **Rogue:** sly, evasive, coin and secrets.
- **Hunter:** tracks, weather, the pet as a companion.
- **Druid:** balance, seasons, the wild, shapes.
- **Shaman:** the elements, spirits, ancestors.

## Personality and mood

Personality tags (`proud`, `shy`, `drunk`, `grieving`) change *how* the voice sounds: sentence length,
confidence, humour. Write a scene per mood if the player wants to switch tone (e.g. `greeting` and
`greetingtired`).
